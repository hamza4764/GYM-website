Meet Your Trainer

Real results, real experience - trained under an award-winning physique athlete.

Mr. Pakistan MenPhysique 2022

Saqib Bashir

Owner and head trainer of Saqib Fitness, Saqib has been a competitive physique athlete since 2016. He has taken the stage in Rawalpindi and Islamabad - from Mr. Capital Zone (RWP) in 2016 and Junior Mr. RWP in 2018 to 1st position at Sr. Mr. Pakistan (65 kg) in 2022 and 2nd place at Mr. Islamabad Physique the same year.

He is a Certified Fitness Trainer (National Fitness Union, 2022) and has completed professional training programs with Getz Pharma Talent Academy and Ashraf Chaudhry Associates. That mix of stage discipline and formal training shapes his coaching: clear plans, honest guidance and a serious, no-excuses environment for both ladies and gents.
